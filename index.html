How it works

From Etsy to a production sheet in under a minute.

  1. 1

    Download your orders

    In Etsy's Shop Manager, download your orders file. Etsy already has a button for it.

  2. 2

    Drop it into Makersheet

    Upload the file. Makersheet pulls out every buyer's personalization exactly as they typed it.

  3. 3

    Print and make

    Get a clean sheet grouped by product, with anything odd flagged. Print it or export it, then start making.

It catches mistakes before they reach the workbench.

Makersheet does one job: making sure what you make matches what the buyer asked for.

  • Copied exactly

    No retyping, so no typos. Capital letters, spacing and symbols stay just as the buyer wrote them.

  • Odd entries flagged

    Blank fields, missing dates, text that's too long or unusual characters get marked for a second look.

  • Grouped by product

    All the necklaces together, all the signs together, so you can make each batch in one go.

  • Your data stays yours

    No Etsy login. You upload only the order file and decide what happens to it.

Questions

Do I have to give you my Etsy password?

No. You download your orders file from Etsy yourself and upload it here. Makersheet never logs in to your shop.

What kinds of products does it work for?

Anything where the buyer types something in: engraved jewelry, printed mugs and shirts, embroidered items, custom signs, name stickers and more.

Does joining the waitlist cost anything?

No. You just leave your email. We'll write when Makersheet is ready, and you decide then whether it's worth it for your shop.

Is Makersheet made by Etsy?

No. Makersheet is an independent tool built for Etsy sellers. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Etsy.
